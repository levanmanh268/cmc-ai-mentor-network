# Demo expense reimbursement guide (fictional)

Expense reimbursement requests must include a receipt and the project cost center. An employee submits requests through the internal expense portal. The simulated finance team reviews supporting invoices before approving eligible items.

For this demonstration, there is no specified reimbursement deadline or payment date. Avoid inventing reimbursement amounts, deadlines, or eligibility rules that are not stated in the source.
